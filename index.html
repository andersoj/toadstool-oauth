Toadstool Sports

This site describes the private YouTube packaging helper used by Toadstool Sports (@LearnToToad) to manage the channel’s own show videos.

The app connects to the Toadstool Sports YouTube channel so we can update titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, and upload Shorts for daily sports-betting shows. It is only used by the channel owner.

Channel: youtube.com/@ToadstoolSports
